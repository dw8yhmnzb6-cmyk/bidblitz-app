### Long Description — English
BidBlitz brings secure wallet payments, QR Pay, POS operations and merchant tools together in one mobile experience designed for both everyday users and growing businesses.

For customers, BidBlitz makes it easy to pay, manage balances, access invoices, send and receive money, and use QR-based payment flows from a single app. Users can view payment activity, manage account settings, access support, and use mobility services such as taxi booking when available in their region.

For merchants and business teams, BidBlitz offers tools to accept wallet payments, run QR Pay checkouts, manage invoicing, operate POS flows, and support staff workflows. Businesses can create professional payment links, track invoice activity, manage operational actions at checkout, and streamline customer payments with a clean, mobile-first interface.

BidBlitz also supports point-of-sale and cashier use cases. Merchants can process payments through QR scanning, customer lookup, PIN-confirmed cashier flows, and business tools built for in-store operations. NFC and RFID-related support is available for supported hardware and permitted use cases, helping enable fast identity and payment interactions where configured.

The wallet experience is focused on secure and transparent payments. Users can manage funds, review balances, confirm payment activity, and access clearly separated flows for private transfers, merchant payments and invoice-based transactions. BidBlitz does not use mock payments in production releases.

For invoicing and business collections, BidBlitz enables merchants to create invoices, payment links and QR-based payment requests to support real-world transactions. These tools are designed to help businesses get paid quickly while keeping the customer experience simple.

Mobility services extend the platform beyond payments. Where enabled, users can access taxi and related mobility functionality directly from the app, including map-based discovery, address search and ride-related flows.

BidBlitz may include rewards and user engagement features, but the mobile store build excludes auctions, mystery boxes, spin-wheel mechanics, Plinko, penny auctions, chance-based paid rewards and gambling-style modules. The store-safe release is focused on secure financial, merchant and mobility use cases.

Privacy and security are core parts of the platform. BidBlitz supports account protection, transaction monitoring, permission-based access to device features such as camera, location, notifications, photos and NFC where relevant, and dedicated privacy controls including account deletion and data deletion request paths.

## 2) Category Recommendation

### Apple App Store
- Recommended primary category: **Business**
- Reason: The mobile store-safe build is centered around merchant workflows, POS, invoicing, QR Pay, staff tools and operational business utilities. Apple review is typically stricter on finance positioning when the app also acts as a merchant operating tool.

### Google Play
- Recommended primary category: **Finance**
- Reason: Wallet, QR Pay, payments, invoices and financial flows are central end-user functions, which align well with Finance on Google Play.

### Practical recommendation
- Apple: Business
- Google Play: Finance
