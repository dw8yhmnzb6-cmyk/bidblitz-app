## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

**Account Information:**
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (encrypted)
- Profile picture (optional)
- Date of birth (for age verification)

**Identity Verification (KYC):**
- Government-issued ID (for certain features)
- Selfie photo (for verification)
- Address proof (if required)

**Payment Information:**
- Wallet balance
- Transaction history
- Payment methods (credit/debit cards, crypto wallets)
- Bank account details (for payouts)

**Business Information (Merchants/Drivers):**
- Business name and registration
- Tax ID
- License numbers (taxi drivers, food vendors)
- Bank account for payouts

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Device Information:**
- Device type, model, and operating system
- Unique device identifiers (UDID, advertising ID)
- Mobile network information
- App version

**Usage Data:**
- Pages/screens viewed
- Features used
- Time spent in app
- Crash reports and error logs

**Location Data:**
- GPS coordinates (for taxi, delivery, scooter services)
- IP address
- Wi-Fi access points
- Cell tower information

We collect location data **only when you use location-based services** (Taxi, Food Delivery, Scooter). You can disable location access in your device settings, but this will limit functionality.

**Cookies & Similar Technologies:**
- Session cookies
- Analytics cookies (Firebase, PostHog)
- Advertising identifiers

### 2.2 Account Management

- Verify your identity (KYC)
- Send notifications about your orders and rides
- Provide customer support

### 2.3 Safety & Security
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
- Detect and prevent abuse
- Monitor for suspicious activity
- Comply with legal obligations

### 2.4 Improvement & Analytics
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve app performance
- Develop new features
- Conduct A/B testing

### 2.5 Marketing & Communications
- Send promotional offers (with your consent)
- Share news and updates
- Conduct surveys
- Personalize your experience

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time via app settings or email links.

We share your information in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Service Providers
- **Drivers & Merchants:** We share your name, phone, pickup/delivery address with drivers and restaurants for order fulfillment
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe, PayPal (for payment processing)
- **Cloud Services:** AWS, MongoDB Atlas (for data storage)
- **Analytics:** Firebase, PostHog (for app analytics)
- **Email Service:** Resend (for transactional emails)

### 3.2 Legal Requirements
- Compliance with laws, regulations, or legal requests
- Protection of our rights and property
- Investigation of fraud or security issues
- Emergency situations (safety concerns)

### 3.3 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you via email and/or app notification.

### 3.4 With Your Consent
We may share information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

**We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.**

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

- **Account Data:** Until you delete your account + 90 days
- **Transaction History:** 7 years (legal requirement)
- **Chat Logs:** 1 year
- **Location Data:** 30 days after ride/order completion
- **Crash Reports:** 90 days

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time via app settings or by contacting privacy@bidblitz.ae.
