## Description

### English
BidBlitz brings wallet payments, QR Pay, POS tools, smart invoices, payment links and business management together in one secure mobile app.

Users can open their wallet, review payment activity, scan or present QR codes for payments, access invoices and manage payment links from a single experience. The app is designed to keep everyday payment actions clear, fast and easy to understand.

Merchants and business teams can use BidBlitz for point-of-sale flows, customer payment handling, invoice collection, staff-related tools and payment operations. The mobile experience supports practical day-to-day workflows with a focus on clarity, privacy and secure transactions.

BidBlitz also includes mobility features where available, including taxi-related discovery and ride support. Device permissions such as camera, location, notifications, photos and NFC are only requested when relevant to the feature a user chooses to use.

Security and privacy are central to the experience. BidBlitz provides visible support access, account controls, secure transport, data deletion paths and business-ready financial utilities in a store-safe mobile build.
