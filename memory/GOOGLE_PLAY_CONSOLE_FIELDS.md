## Full Description

### English
BidBlitz combines wallet payments, QR Pay, POS tools, smart invoices, payment links, merchant workflows and mobility support in one secure mobile app.

Users can review wallet activity, open QR-based payment flows, access invoices, manage payment links and reach support and privacy controls from one place. The app is designed to keep payments clear, practical and easy to understand.

Merchants and business teams can use BidBlitz for point-of-sale operations, customer payment handling, invoice collection, staff-related workflows and business management tasks. The mobile experience is focused on real-world utility, privacy and secure transaction handling.

BidBlitz also includes mobility and taxi-related features where available. Device permissions such as camera, location, notifications, NFC and photos are requested only when relevant to the feature a user chooses to use.
